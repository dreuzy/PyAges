# Scientific Reports and Change Records

These documents record numerical qualifications, scientific migrations, and
methodological decisions that affect interpretation or reproducibility. They
are evidence for reviewers and maintainers, not step-by-step user guides.

Each report states its scope and date. Generated or machine-specific evidence
may live outside the versioned documentation; the report must identify where
that evidence can be reproduced.
